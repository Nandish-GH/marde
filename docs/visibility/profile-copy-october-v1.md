## LinkedIn About

MARDE Inc. is a New Jersey-based, concept-stage emergency-response robotics developer.

We are investigating how an aerial-and-ground platform could bring useful equipment and scene information toward an incident before EMS arrives. Our October 2026 V1 direction combines:

- MARDE Air: proposed transport, controlled hover lowering and aerial scene views.
- MARDE Ground: outdoor teleoperation, an AED bay and tourniquet dispenser, video and two-way communication.
- MARDE Nexus: proposed scene feeds, supply status and human authorization of deployment, dispensing and retreat.

Initial demonstrations are planned with training equipment and simulated incidents. Indoor entry, stair climbing and direct robotic treatment are outside V1. The next work is supply sizing, Ground mass planning and dispenser bench tests before staged integration.

MARDE is pre-prototype and pre-revenue. We have no completed hardware, demonstrated missions, deployed systems, commercial pilots or validated full-system results to report. We do not claim clinical validation, operational flight approval or production 911 integration.

We welcome requirements feedback and technical critique from EMS, public-safety teams, engineers, researchers and potential advisers. A research conversation does not imply a partnership or endorsement.
